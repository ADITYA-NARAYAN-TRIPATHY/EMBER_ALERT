Coral Metropolis Framework

A sponge-like urban resilience architecture integrating nature-based solutions with AI-powered hydrological engineering

Floodable Linear Parks

Public urban spaces engineered to temporarily absorb excess monsoon runoff, safely impounding stormwater away from high-density housing.

Networked Rain Gardens

Strategically planted Bioswale depressions that capture surface runoff, filter heavy urban sediments, and ease drainage pressure.

Green Roofs & Vertical Forests

Retrofitting commercial and residential architecture with living sponge layers to retention-absorb rainwater before it reaches street channels.

Permeable Pavements

Replacing impervious concrete with porous stone matrices, allowing surface water to recharge natural aquifer tables.

AI-Powered Drainage System

Automated telemetry sensors and predictive algorithms adjusting discharge gates based on transboundary cloudburst data.

Safe Conveyance Channels

Engineered diversion canals directing excess floodwaters safely out of riverine cities while mimicking natural hydrological flows.
